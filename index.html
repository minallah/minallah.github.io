    About Me

    I am a scientist at the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR), US. My research interests are broadly interdisciplinary, spanning multiple domains of Earth system science — including the atmosphere, cryosphere, hydrosphere, and geosphere — and involve the use of numerical models, climate data analysis, and GIS tools. I have previously worked as a civil engineer and water resources professional in Pakistan.

    Education
